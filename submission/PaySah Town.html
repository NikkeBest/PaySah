  • Not a duplicate, money request or scheduled transfer
  • 金额等于订单应付金额

The Owner approves or rejects with a reason, for example order DEMO-A-780 at RM 780. The Owner cannot override missing bank evidence: partial payments, wrong accounts, amount mismatches and missing bank records stay unpaid.

店主填写理由后批准或拒绝，例如 RM 780 的订单 DEMO-A-780。店主也不能代替缺失的银行证据：部分付款、转错账户、金额不符、没有入账的单都保持未付。

Only a fully paid order can be released, and orders above RM500 need the Owner. Every step, who did it, when and why, is saved in the activity log.

只有全额付款的订单能放行，超过 RM500 需要店主放行。每一步谁做的、何时、为什么，都记录在活动日志里。
